Measured, then disclosed Mandate 04 / 05
9hostile attacks 9blocked 0escaped 0vacuous

Every attack runs twice. Once against a deliberately unhardened gateway whose only job is to prove the attack is real, and once against the real one. If that witness never fires, the attack scores vacuous and counts as nothing. A suite that blocks nine attacks which were never possible is the exact bug this exists to catch.

Read the baseline as "clearly below 100%", not as 44.4%. Three families bootstrapped gives a 95% interval of [0%, 100%]. The number carrying information is the noisy one.
